# Discovered attacks

In a **discovered attack**, one piece moves out of the way and **uncovers** an attack by a piece standing behind it. Two pieces attack at once: the one that moved, and the one revealed.

When the uncovered attack is a **check**, it's a **discovered check**. The opponent must answer the check, so the piece that moved is free to grab almost anything.

::: example
fen: 4k3/8/8/2q5/4N3/8/8/4R2K w - - 0 1
moves: e4c5
caption: The knight blocks the rook's line to the black king. Nxc5+ takes the queen and uncovers the rook's check along the e-file at the same time. Black must deal with the check and can't recapture.
:::

## How to look for discovered attacks

Look for your own piece standing between one of your long-range pieces (bishop, rook, queen) and an enemy target, especially the king. Then ask: where can the piece in front go to do damage of its own? A capture, a check, or an attack on the queen makes the discovery decisive.
